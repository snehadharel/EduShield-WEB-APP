Who We Are

EduShield is an AI-powered academic portal built for schools and colleges. We connect students, teachers, and administrators on one secure platform for courses, exams, attendance, and more.

Our Mission

To empower every learner with easy access to academic resources while protecting institutional data through intelligent, adaptive security that adapts to real-world threats.

Our Vision

A future where technology and security work together — where students focus on learning, teachers on teaching, and AI silently guards every login and every session.

Platform Features

📚 Academic Portal Courses, assignments, exams, attendance, grades, notices, and class routines — all in one place.
🛡️ AI Intrusion Detection ML-based risk scoring, adaptive OTP, geolocation checks, and SHAP explainability for every login.
⏱️ Online Timed Exams Teachers create MCQ exams; students take them with a live countdown and automatic grading.
🔐 Security Center Students review login history, manage trusted devices, and report suspicious activity.
